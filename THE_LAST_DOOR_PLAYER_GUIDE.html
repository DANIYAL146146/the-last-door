THE LAST DOOR

Every door hides a secret.

MYSTERY • ADVENTURE • PUZZLES

Welcome, Explorer!

You have entered a mysterious world filled with secrets, hidden objects and strange discoveries.

Your mission is to explore twelve chapters, find keys, unlock doors and discover the truth behind the house.

How to Play

Explore each room, find the golden key and unlock the exit door.

Desktop Controls

KeyAction
W / ↑Move up
S / ↓Move down
A / ←Move left
D / →Move right
E / SPACEInteract
ESCPause

Mobile Controls

Use the directional buttons to move and the INTERACT button to open the door.

Your Mission

  1. Explore the environment.
  2. Find the hidden key.
  3. Avoid obstacles.
  4. Approach the exit door.
  5. Press E or SPACE to unlock it.
  6. Discover the chapter mystery.
  7. Continue your adventure.

The Twelve Chapters

Helpful Tips

  • Explore every corner.
  • Look for golden objects.
  • Remember mysterious messages.
  • Watch for hidden paths.
  • Take your time.

Saving Your Progress

Your current chapter is saved automatically in your browser using localStorage.

Choose CONTINUE from the main menu to resume.

Clearing browser storage can erase saved progress.
