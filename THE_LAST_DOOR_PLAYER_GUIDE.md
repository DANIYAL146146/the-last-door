
# THE LAST DOOR
## Official Player Guide

Welcome to THE LAST DOOR!

A mysterious adventure awaits you. Explore strange worlds, discover hidden clues, solve puzzles and uncover the secrets behind twelve mysterious doors.

## HOW TO PLAY

Your mission is to explore each world, find important objects and unlock the exit door.

### Desktop Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W / Arrow Up | Move up |
| S / Arrow Down | Move down |
| A / Arrow Left | Move left |
| D / Arrow Right | Move right |
| E / Space | Interact |
| Escape | Pause |

### Mobile Controls

- Use the directional buttons to move.
- Press INTERACT to open doors and interact with objects.

## YOUR MAIN OBJECTIVES

1. Explore the environment.
2. Find the hidden key.
3. Avoid obstacles.
4. Approach the exit door.
5. Press E or SPACE to unlock it when you have the key.
6. Discover the chapter's mystery.
7. Continue to the next chapter.

## HELPFUL TIPS

- Explore carefully.
- Look for glowing objects.
- Read mysterious messages.
- Remember clues.
- Explore around obstacles.
- Don't rush through the story.

## SAVING YOUR PROGRESS

Your progress is saved automatically in your browser using localStorage.

Use CONTINUE from the main menu to resume your saved chapter.

Important: Clearing browser data may erase your saved progress.
